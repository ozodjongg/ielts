# Data and Licensing

## English bank

The bundled English assessment bank is the validated 8,000-item bank carried forward into the IELTS server-owned bank format: 80 subjects × 100 variants, with the answer model separate from the public question representation.

## SAT Math

The SAT bank in this release is generated original practice content. It is not copied from College Board exams, Bluebook, Question Bank, or another copyrighted test source. `source=original_generated_v5` is stored on every row.

The name SAT is used descriptively for test-preparation compatibility. Do not represent the product as endorsed by College Board.

## Vocabulary

A trustworthy 100,000-entry English→Uzbek learner dictionary requires a source with clear commercial/redistribution rights plus CEFR/frequency/synonym metadata. IELTS therefore separates **software capacity** from **corpus claim**.

Bundled production lexemes: **0**. Bundled smoke-test lexemes: see `data/vocabulary/MANIFEST.json`.

Every imported production row must contain `source_name` and `source_license`. Keep attribution/source references required by your license. Do not scrape a public dictionary and assume commercial redistribution is allowed.

## Listening audio

IELTS ships no copyrighted songs/recordings. A learning center must upload audio it owns, created itself, or is licensed to use. The product should retain licensing/asset provenance operationally if commercial audio libraries are introduced.
